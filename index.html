      Aging: → Level 4 after 120 min
      Anti-Starvation Aging — patients waiting too long are automatically promoted one level:
      • Level 5 → Level 4 after 120 minutes
      • Level 4 → Level 3 after 90 minutes at that level
      • Level 3 → Level 2 after 60 minutes at that level
      • Maximum promotion ceiling: Level 2 (never Level 1)

      Treatment Order: Always the highest-priority non-empty queue first. Within the same level: strict FIFO by arrival time.
